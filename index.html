Something rotten is happening in the sewers

Dr. Verminbane spent years studying the rats beneath the city and decided they're far too clever. Now he wants them gone, and he's got your cheese.

Your guide MacSqueak sends you to Rusty's Hideout to build a crew and fight back, one stage and one boss at a time.

What you'll be doing

The cast

Collect 55 rat heroes

From Ratcula and Edgar Allan Paw to Isaac Gnewton and Ratputin. Every rat has three skills that unlock as it levels, plus a Leader Aura that buffs the whole team.

Team building

Build the perfect crew

Mix four roles and five elements with a weakness wheel, then pick a leader to power up allies who share their element.

Idle progress

Your rats never clock out

Your crew keeps battling while you're away. Come back to a bag full of loot, spend it on upgrades, and push deeper.

Recruiting

Dig through Rusty's Hideout

Search the garbage pile for a free daily recruit, or tune in to Rusty's radio for rarer rats.

Endless dungeon

Conquer the CheddarCombs

Head down floor after floor of an endless cave system, gather materials and craft armor from Leather all the way to Voidsteel.

Competition

Climb the Arena

Fight your way up the Arena leagues from Bronze to Diamond and earn rewards you can't get anywhere else.

Progression

Rank up and Ascend

Take your rats through five star tiers from Bronze to Ultimate, level them to 250, and Ascend to start the climb again even stronger.

Every day

Always something to do

Daily, weekly and monthly tasks, a login calendar, and a world chat where you can team up with other scavengers.

Early testing. Idle Rats is still being built and balanced. Progress may be reset as the game improves.
